Two-Stage Evaluation Pipeline

Stage 1 (Hard Eligibility Gates): Every candidate provider must pass 4 non-negotiable gates: Technology overlap (at least 1 requested technology supplied), Operating scale capacity bounds (target kW falls inside provider [min_kw, max_kw]), Building sector compatibility, and minimum India/Pune service presence.

Stage 2 (Multi-Criteria Compatibility Scoring): Eligible candidates receive an audit-grade score (0–100): S_total = max(0, Σ(w_i * s_i) - P_missing) where weights cover Technology Fit (25%), Capacity Fit (20%), Service (20%), Certifications (15%), Documentation (10%), and Budget (10%).
